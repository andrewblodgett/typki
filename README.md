# typki

## why
I had claude make this for me to harness the power of two great pieces of software. Anki, the king of spaced review flashcards, has an incredible algorithm for helping me memorize stuff for eternity. It has atrocious UI though, and the majority of the stuff I want to remember is math/physics stuff that is a pain to type out in LaTeX, especially at in-class speeds. Typst is basically just LaTeX but actually pleasant to use and much faster and better in every way, but lacks support in Anki because of its novelty.

There are already a couple projects out there that integrate typst into anki, but none of them were exactly what I wanted. But luckily tokens are cheap, so claude cooked up this little app where you can do all your flashcards directly in typst and it will save them as SVG into your anki deck.
